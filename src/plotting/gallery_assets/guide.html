My decision lens

Five questions before capital

  1. 01Can this direction actually be traded through the broker on that day?
  2. 02Does the pair stay positive under the 30 bps cost test?
  3. 03Is the sample comparable, liquid, and supported by enough trades?
  4. 04Can the planned position survive the displayed drawdown?
  5. 05Has the rule passed paper trading without changing the entry or exit?
What this research does not prove

It does not prove future profitability, guaranteed fills, short availability, or the ability to compound at the displayed historical rate. Use the ranking to decide what deserves more testing.
