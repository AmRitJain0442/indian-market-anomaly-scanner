1, 3, and 5 sessions

Short-horizon forecast

PRIMARY PAPER

A shared cross-sectional model pools observations across stocks. This project adapts the paper to daily NSE data with a transparent ridge regression.

LEAKAGE CONTROL

Train strictly before each test date

The 60-session held-out test refits every 20 sessions. Every training outcome ends before the next test block starts.

INTERPRETATION

No verified predictive edge

Direction accuracy is close to 51 percent and out-of-sample R squared is negative. These forecasts are diagnostics, not recommendations.

Held-out evidence

Validation by horizon

The range target is 80 percent. Negative R squared means the model lost to a zero-return forecast on squared error.

My decision lens

Five questions before capital

  1. 01Can this direction actually be traded through the broker on that day?
  2. 02Does the pair stay positive under the 30 bps cost test?
  3. 03Is the sample comparable, liquid, and supported by enough trades?
  4. 04Can the planned position survive the displayed drawdown?
  5. 05Has the rule passed paper trading without changing the entry or exit?
What this research does not prove

It does not prove future profitability, guaranteed fills, short availability, or the ability to compound at the displayed historical rate. Use the ranking to decide what deserves more testing.
