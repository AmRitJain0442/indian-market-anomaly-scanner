Reinvestment rule

Yesterday’s closing equity becomes today’s sizing base. Gains increase the next position automatically. Losses reduce it. We reinvest the account, but never expose the whole account. Half-size uses 25% notional and 0.25% risk. Full pilot uses 50% notional and 0.5% risk.
