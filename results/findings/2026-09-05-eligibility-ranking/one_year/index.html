Screening rules and how to use this list
  1. Use this list to select a stock-strategy pair for further testing, not as a buy or sell signal.
  2. The practical view requires current EQ, zero non-EQ historical trade legs, a dated Zerodha MIS listing, price at least INR 100 and one whole share within the cash cap and the illustrative planned-risk limit.
  3. It also requires 20 recent sessions with median daily turnover at least INR 50 crore, 95% historical coverage and at least 20 historical trades. Overnight strategies need a separate delivery review.
  4. The INR 100 floor and liquidity limit are conservative pilot choices, not exchange prohibitions. Use the other views to inspect excluded stocks and their reasons.
  5. Risk shares use a tick-rounded 1% stop, estimated charges and a 0.20% adverse-fill allowance with a two-tick minimum per share. The worse of long and short planned loss must fit INR 5. This is a sizing illustration, not a newly backtested stop rule or a guaranteed loss cap.
  6. Neither share count is an order quantity. Check current quotes, charges, stop distance and your risk budget before sizing. No leverage is assumed even when the broker offers it.
  7. Historical figures come from a different, theoretical reference account. Initial-capital drawdown is corrected, but exact opening fills, retrospective exclusions and selection bias remain unresolved.
  8. Recheck exchange and broker permissions each session. This page does not connect to Zerodha or Dhan and does not place orders.
